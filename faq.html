F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What does Freixo supply?

    Freixo supplies a wide range of everyday essentials for homes, businesses and projects, including tissue & paper products, disposable & food packaging, cleaning & hygiene products, polythene & waste solutions, PPE & safety supplies, and selected building materials.

  • Do you supply both wholesale and retail quantities?

    Yes. We serve both wholesale and retail customers, with quantities depending on the product and requirement.

  • Who can buy from Freixo?

    We supply homes, families, restaurants & cafés, offices, hotels, cleaning companies, contractors, retailers, schools and other businesses and institutions across Qatar.

  • Do you deliver across Qatar?

    Yes. We provide delivery for customers across Qatar, subject to the order, location and delivery arrangement.

  • Is there a minimum order for delivery?

    Delivery arrangements may vary depending on the order size, location and delivery route. Contact us with your requirements and we’ll advise you on the available option.

  • Can I order regular or monthly supplies?

    Yes. We can support regular and recurring supply requirements for businesses, households and other customers. Advance ordering can also be arranged for recurring requirements.

  • Can Freixo source products that are not listed on the website?

    Yes. If you require a specific product, specification or quantity that is not currently listed, contact us with your requirements and we will check the available sourcing options.

  • Do you supply customized products?

    Yes. Selected products such as bags, paper cups and other packaging items can be sourced with customization, depending on the required specification and quantity.

  • How can I request a quotation?

    You can use our Request a Quote form on the website or contact us directly through WhatsApp, phone or email. Please share the product, quantity, specification and delivery location where possible.
