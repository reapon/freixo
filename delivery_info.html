Delivery Information

🚚 DELIVERY INFORMATION


Qatar-Wide Delivery

Freixo provides delivery across Qatar for household, business and project requirements. Delivery arrangements may vary depending on the order size, delivery location and route availability.

Scheduled Delivery

To make delivery more convenient and efficient, we organize deliveries based on area and planned delivery routes. Customers may receive their orders on a scheduled route day.

Regular & Monthly Orders

For customers with regular or monthly requirements, we can arrange recurring delivery schedules based on their preferred requirements and route availability. Advance orders can be placed to help us prepare your items in time.

Small Orders & Pick-Up

For smaller orders or selected low-value packs, store collection may be available. If your order falls within a planned delivery route, delivery may also be arranged subject to route availability.

Urgent Delivery

Urgent or special deliveries outside our planned routes may be subject to additional delivery charges, depending on the location and requirement.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges, if applicable, will depend on the order value, delivery location and delivery arrangement. We will confirm the applicable delivery option before proceeding with the order.

Need Delivery Assistance?

Share your order details and delivery location with us, and our team will help arrange the most suitable delivery option.
